The WOFF2 files in this directory are unmodified Google Fonts distributions of Cairo, Montserrat and Tajawal, downloaded from fonts.gstatic.com on 2026-09-05. Original versioned filenames are retained for cache safety. Each family's copyright and SIL Open Font License are included in its `*-OFL.txt` file.

The corresponding font-face declarations and unicode ranges are in `app/fonts.css`. Keep versioned filenames when updating fonts. The requested weights remain Cairo/Montserrat 500, 600, 700 and Tajawal 400, 500, 700; `font-display: swap` preserves text visibility.
